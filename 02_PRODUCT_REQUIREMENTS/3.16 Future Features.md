## 1. Overview

This document defines the future product roadmap for FANNAN beyond the current MVP and core marketplace scope. It organizes innovation initiatives into roadmap phases and provides a consistent structure for each proposed feature, including description, business value, complexity, dependencies, priority, and suggested release timing.

The roadmap intentionally separates foundational product maturity from expansion opportunities. The features in this document are not assumed to be required for the initial marketplace launch and should only be pursued when the platform has sufficient operational readiness, product-market fit, and technical capacity.

The roadmap is grouped into a four-phase progression:

- Phase 2: Growth and engagement expansion
- Phase 3: Premium and platform experiences
- Phase 4: Strategic platform and ecosystem scale

This document is product-level planning guidance only. It does not define database schema, API contracts, data models, or implementation architecture.

## 2. Roadmap Philosophy

| Principle | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Start with core trust | Advanced features must not undermine product reliability, payout trust, or customer confidence. |
| Release by readiness | Features move forward when inventory, operations, moderation, and support readiness are in place. |
| Keep user value first | Innovation must deepen artist monetization, customer experience, or marketplace utility. |
| Preserve platform boundaries | Features must respect FANNAN’s business model, compliance posture, and moderation capabilities. |
| Use phased validation | Each opportunity should be validated with pilot cohorts, operational readiness checks, and measurable business KPIs. |

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

## 7. Suggested Roadmap Sequencing

1. Phase 2: validate engagement and recurring revenue opportunities with AI recommendations, artist subscriptions, live streaming, and curated virtual gallery experiences.
2. Phase 3: expand premium and platform capabilities through internationalization, AR preview, API and SDK exposure, and B2B opportunities once core marketplace operations are mature.
3. Phase 4: scale into loyalty, gifting, and multi-vendor logistics to support long-term platform growth and broader operational maturity.

## 8. Release Governance

- Each feature must pass a documented readiness review before release.
- Business value, operational cost, and support capacity must be validated before scaling a feature beyond pilot release.
- Experiments should use measurable success criteria instead of being launched as open-ended product bets.
- Features with high operational risk or legal complexity should be gated under tighter review and narrower rollout boundaries.

## 9. Acceptance Summary

- The roadmap is organized by phases and prioritizes customer growth, artist monetization, and platform scale.
- Each feature is defined with the required business and product context: description, business value, complexity, dependencies, priority, and suggested release.
- The roadmap balances innovation with operational readiness and supports phased expansion beyond the initial marketplace foundation.
