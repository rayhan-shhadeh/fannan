## 1. Scope and Conventions

This glossary defines the shared vocabulary for the FANNAN platform. It uses business-first language, with software context where relevant to clarify impact on APIs, data modeling, dashboards, and workflows.

The glossary is intentionally written to avoid unnecessary implementation detail. It distinguishes between business meaning and engineering interpretation while still noting the likely system effects.

### 1.1 Terminology Principles

- Use the same term the same way everywhere.
- Prefer product language over internal jargon.
- Keep names stable across website, app, dashboard, and admin tools.
- Use related-term cross references to resolve ambiguity.
- Treat this as the canonical vocabulary for product documents, UI text, QA, and technical design.

## 4. Term Definitions

### Platform

#### Name
Platform

#### Definition
The complete FANNAN ecosystem, including the public website, mobile application, artist dashboard, customer dashboard, admin dashboard, and supporting services.

#### Purpose
To unify the user experience and product rules across every access point.

#### Used By
Customer, Artist, Admin, Support Agent, System

#### Related Terms
Marketplace, Dashboard, Public Website, Mobile Application, API Platform

#### Database Impact
Likely spans multiple core domains, including users, artworks, orders, payments, notifications, analytics, and settings.

#### API Impact
Platform-level APIs may expose account, marketplace, dashboard, and admin resources; all functional domains must behave consistently across surfaces.

#### UI Impact
Web and mobile surfaces share the same navigation, product states, and messaging patterns.

#### Business Rules
- All user roles operate within the same core ruleset.
- Platform features must remain consistent across Arabic and English experiences.
- Platform-level permissions and governance apply to all surfaces.

#### Notes
This is the umbrella term for the whole product and should not be confused with a single user interface or feature.

### Marketplace

#### Name
Marketplace

#### Definition
The public commercial layer of FANNAN where buyers discover, evaluate, save, follow, and purchase eligible artworks and artist offerings.

#### Purpose
To create a trusted destination for art discovery and commerce.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin, Moderator

#### Related Terms
Platform, Digital Gallery, Search, Recommendation, Product, Collection, Category

#### Database Impact
Likely connected to products, categories, collections, tags, inventory, and user interactions.

#### API Impact
Marketplace listing, search, browse, filter, detail, favorite, follow, and recommendation endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Homepage, search results, category pages, collection pages, product detail pages, and related content modules.

#### Business Rules
- Marketplace content must be public, eligible, and approved according to policy.
- Drafts, private content, and suspended listings must remain hidden from public discovery.
- Product authenticity and visibility rules apply before a listing is shown.

#### Notes
The marketplace is not only the buying flow; it is the entire public discovery and evaluation layer.

### Digital Gallery

#### Name
Digital Gallery

#### Definition
A curated, digital presentation space where artworks, collections, and artist stories are showcased in a premium browsing experience.

#### Purpose
To provide discoverability and editorial presentation beyond a plain catalogue.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Marketplace, Portfolio, Featured Collection, Virtual Exhibition, Artwork

#### Database Impact
Likely stores gallery states, featured arrangements, collection membership, and gallery metadata.

#### API Impact
Gallery listing, collection detail, featured content, and editorial endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Homepage hero sections, curated collections, featured artworks, artist pages, and showcase modules.

#### Business Rules
- Content in a digital gallery must be approved and relevant.
- Galleries may highlight selected works without changing the artist’s original product availability.
- Curated presentation must not misrepresent the work or create false scarcity.

#### Notes
A digital gallery can be a curated editorial experience or a collection-driven showcase.

### Portfolio

#### Name
Portfolio

#### Definition
The artist’s public presentation of their work, materials, practice, and story, used to communicate identity and value.

#### Purpose
To give artists a professional identity and help buyers understand the work beyond the listing.

#### Used By
Artist, Customer, Admin, Moderator

#### Related Terms
Artist Profile, Artwork, Artwork Story, Collection, Digital Gallery

#### Database Impact
Likely linked to artists, artworks, media, profile metadata, bio, and public visibility settings.

#### API Impact
Profile read and update endpoints, portfolio listing, and artwork retrieval APIs.

#### UI Impact
Artist profile page, portfolio tabs, gallery views, and artwork index pages.

#### Business Rules
- Portfolio content cannot be published without an active artist profile in an eligible state.
- Public portfolio content must respect visibility rules and moderation status.
- An artist’s portfolio is distinct from their sellable catalog, though they may overlap.

#### Notes
A portfolio is not just a product list; it is the artist’s identity and narrative context.

### Dashboard

#### Name
Dashboard

#### Definition
A role-specific control area where users manage activity, settings, content, orders, and operational tasks.

#### Purpose
To enable role-based management for customers, artists, and admins.

#### Used By
Customer, Artist, Admin, Moderator, Support Agent

#### Related Terms
Artist Dashboard, Customer Dashboard, Admin Dashboard, Dashboard Metrics, Activity Log

#### Database Impact
Likely uses access-controlled entities for profiles, order history, content, notifications, and reports.

#### API Impact
Role-specific dashboard resources, summary endpoints, filtering, and reporting APIs.

#### UI Impact
Summary panels, tables, actions, filters, analytics cards, and history views.

#### Business Rules
- Dashboards must display only the data the user is authorized to view.
- Summary metrics must reflect authoritative backend state.
- User actions must respect boundary conditions for sensitive operational data.

#### Notes
Dashboard refers to the concept, not a single page; a dashboard may contain multiple modules.

### Landing Page

#### Name
Landing Page

#### Definition
A marketing or entry page designed to introduce the platform, showcase value, and guide users toward discovery or sign-up.

#### Purpose
To communicate FANNAN’s value proposition and direct users into the right journey.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Homepage, Brand, Search, Product, Collection

#### Database Impact
May include metadata, campaign associations, editorial content, and featured content references.

#### API Impact
Landing-page content endpoints and editorial configuration APIs.

#### UI Impact
Home entry points, hero sections, call-to-action blocks, and curated feature cards.

#### Business Rules
- Landing pages must not imply unavailable functionality.
- Links must lead to valid public or authenticated flows.
- Any promotional claim must be accurate and compliant with platform rules.

#### Notes
Landing pages can be broader than the home page and may be campaign-specific.

### Collection

#### Name
Collection

#### Definition
A curated grouping of artworks, products, or artist stories assembled around a common theme, narrative, or editorial idea.

#### Purpose
To improve discovery and storytelling across the marketplace.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Category, Tag, Featured Collection, Trending Collection, Portfolio

#### Database Impact
Likely connects collection records to artworks, artists, and editorial metadata.

#### API Impact
Collection listing, collection detail, and membership endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Collection pages, homepage modules, and search results.

#### Business Rules
- Collection membership must respect product visibility and review rules.
- A collection may hold public items only.
- Curated collections must not mislead viewers about the product being part of a special or limited inventory unless that is true.

#### Notes
Collections are an editorial and discovery concept, not an inventory mechanism.

### Category

#### Name
Category

#### Definition
A high-level taxonomy used to organize products, artworks, and marketplace content into recognizable groups.

#### Purpose
To support navigation, browse, filtering, and discovery.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Collection, Tag, Artwork Category, Search

#### Database Impact
Likely stored as taxonomy entities with parent-child or flat hierarchy references.

#### API Impact
Category listing, category detail, and browse-by-category endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Sidebar filters, browse pages, card labels, landing-page modules, and search facets.

#### Business Rules
- Categories must be consistent with product taxonomy and marketplace policy.
- Only approved categories should appear in public navigation.
- A product may belong to one or more categories only if policy allows it.

#### Notes
Categories are broader classification concepts, while tags provide more granular descriptive metadata.

### Tag

#### Name
Tag

#### Definition
A descriptive keyword or label used to classify artworks, collections, or content with more detail than a broad category.

#### Purpose
To improve search relevance, discovery, and filtering.

#### Used By
Customer, Artist, Admin, Search System

#### Related Terms
Category, Artwork Tag, Search, Recommendation

#### Database Impact
Likely appears in metadata tables or many-to-many associations with artwork and collection records.

#### API Impact
Tag retrieval, search facets, and content filtering endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Tag chips, search suggestions, content metadata, and filtering controls.

#### Business Rules
- Tags must be relevant and not misleading.
- Public tags must align with approved value and content policy.
- Duplicate or conflicting tag terms should be normalized.

#### Notes
Tags are descriptive metadata; they are not product inventory attributes by themselves.

### Featured Collection

#### Name
Featured Collection

#### Definition
A collection intentionally highlighted to surface notable work, an artist milestone, or a curated market theme.

#### Purpose
To elevate editorial discovery and support marketplace storytelling.

#### Used By
Admin, Artist, Customer, Guest

#### Related Terms
Collection, Digital Gallery, Trending Collection, Homepage

#### Database Impact
Likely stores feature state, ordering, scheduling, and association with collection records.

#### API Impact
Featured-content endpoints and admin configuration APIs.

#### UI Impact
Homepage, gallery modules, promotional areas, and editorial carousels.

#### Business Rules
- Feature placement must be intentional and governed by editorial or admin rules.
- Featured collections must be visible only when eligible and active.
- Paid promotion must be clear and disclosed when applicable.

#### Notes
Featured collections should be senior editorial choices, not random or algorithmic noise.

### Trending Collection

#### Name
Trending Collection

#### Definition
A collection or group of works showing current popularity, engagement, or demand patterns in the marketplace.

#### Purpose
To provide social proof and support discovery without requiring direct search.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Featured Collection, Search Analytics, Recommendation, Dashboard Metrics

#### Database Impact
Likely derived from analytics events, engagement metrics, and content associations.

#### API Impact
Trending endpoints and ranking or aggregation services.

#### UI Impact
Homepage modules, discovery surfaces, and category-related suggestions.

#### Business Rules
- Trending results must be based on valid activity and should not fabricate popularity.
- Terms such as “trending” must reflect current evidence and be updated from live data.

#### Notes
Trending is an insight-driven feature and should not be confused with “featured.”

### Homepage

#### Name
Homepage

#### Definition
The main entry page for the public marketplace experience.

#### Purpose
To introduce the platform, orient users, and route them to discovery, search, or account actions.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Landing Page, Search, Collection, Featured Collection, Digital Gallery

#### Database Impact
May rely on navigation configuration, featured content, search metadata, and editorial modules.

#### API Impact
Homepage content endpoints, section configuration, and public content assembly APIs.

#### UI Impact
Hero sections, collections, category blocks, search, recommendation panels, and call-to-action buttons.

#### Business Rules
- Only public and eligible content may appear.
- Empty states must be handled gracefully.
- The homepage must remain useful even when catalogue coverage is limited.

#### Notes
The homepage is one of the most important discovery surfaces and should remain clear, premium, and informative.

### Search

#### Name
Search

#### Definition
The user-driven process of locating artworks, artists, products, collections, categories, and related content using keywords and filters.

#### Purpose
To support efficient discovery and reduce the effort required to find relevant content.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Search Analytics, Recommendation, Tag, Category, Homepage, Filter

#### Database Impact
Likely relies on searchable metadata, indices, tags, product data, artist data, and analytics events.

#### API Impact
Search endpoints, autocomplete endpoints, suggestions endpoints, and filter resources.

#### UI Impact
Search bars, filters, sort menus, result pages, and empty states.

#### Business Rules
- Search results must reflect only eligible public and authorized content.
- Query behavior must respect language and localization needs.
- Search ranking must prioritize relevance, quality, and trust.

#### Notes
Search is foundational to discovery; it should support bilingual behavior and clear relevance signals.

### Recommendation

#### Name
Recommendation

#### Definition
A system or editorial suggestion intended to help users discover relevant content based on interest, behavior, or curated content strategy.

#### Purpose
To increase discovery, engagement, and conversion without forcing direct navigation.

#### Used By
Guest, Customer, Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Search, Trending Collection, Featured Collection, Customer Analytics, Personalization

#### Database Impact
Likely depends on interactions, product metadata, user behavior, and ranking policies.

#### API Impact
Recommendation endpoints and ranking services.

#### UI Impact
Homepage tiles, related products, “you may also like,” and artist-aligned suggestions.

#### Business Rules
- Recommendations must not mislead users or hide important product information.
- Sensitive or unrelevant recommendations must be suppressed.
- Recommendation logic must not replace actual product availability checks.

#### Notes
Recommendation is a product feature, not a guarantee of demand or suitability.

### Guest

#### Name
Guest

#### Definition
An unidentified user browsing the public marketplace without creating an account or logging in.

#### Purpose
To allow product discovery and marketing exposure without requiring registration.

#### Used By
Guest, System

#### Related Terms
Registered User, Customer, Login, Session, Landing Page, Homepage

#### Database Impact
Guest activity may be tracked only where consent, privacy, and product rules allow.

#### API Impact
Public browsing and read-only endpoints are generally accessible to guests.

#### UI Impact
Homepage, product pages, category pages, and public profile pages.

#### Business Rules
- Guests must not access private account or artist management features.
- Guest flows must preserve safe commerce entry points and redirect to registration or login when needed.

#### Notes
A guest is not a customer unless they complete registration or account activation.

### Registered User

#### Name
Registered User

#### Definition
A user who has created an account on FANNAN and has an authenticated user profile.

#### Purpose
To enable persistent account access, personalization, saved actions, and commerce activity.

#### Used By
Customer, Artist, Admin, Moderator, Support Agent

#### Related Terms
Guest, Login, Registration, Role, Permission, Session

#### Database Impact
Likely associated with identity, profile, permissions, order history, saved items, and notification settings.

#### API Impact
Authentication and account-related endpoints plus permission-bound resources.

#### UI Impact
Account pages, dashboards, saved lists, settings, and personalization modules.

#### Business Rules
- Registered users must satisfy verification and eligibility requirements for their role.
- Account access must be protected by secure authentication and authorization rules.

#### Notes
This is the general umbrella user type before a more specific role is assigned.

### Customer

#### Name
Customer

#### Definition
A registered buyer or collector using FANNAN to discover, evaluate, purchase, or commission art.

#### Purpose
To enable commerce, collection, and customer relationship management.

#### Used By
Customer

#### Related Terms
Registered User, Artist, Order, Wishlist, Follow, Favorite, Checkout

#### Database Impact
Likely connected to cart, wishlist, orders, reviews, favorites, addresses, and notifications.

#### API Impact
Customer-account, checkout, order, wishlist, favorite, review, and notification endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Customer dashboard, wishlist, order history, checkout flow, saved addresses, and profile settings.

#### Business Rules
- Customers may purchase only eligible items and must satisfy authorization checks.
- Customer data must remain private and protected from unauthorized access.

#### Notes
A customer may also be an artist acting in a buyer role; the role is contextual.

### Artist

#### Name
Artist

#### Definition
A creator, maker, or artisan who publishes work, builds a profile, and may sell or offer commissions through FANNAN.

#### Purpose
To represent the professional creative identity behind the work and provide tools for selling and managing their portfolio.

#### Used By
Artist, Admin

#### Related Terms
Verified Artist, Artist Profile, Artwork, Portfolio, Commission, Order

#### Database Impact
Likely connected to profile data, catalogue, media, orders, payouts, analytics, and communication records.

#### API Impact
Artist profile endpoints, listing management, order management, and analytics resources.

#### UI Impact
Artist dashboard, portfolio management, product publishing controls, order inbox, and performance analytics.

#### Business Rules
- Artists must meet eligibility and verification requirements for public selling access where required.
- Artist actions must remain restricted to their own approved content and resources.

#### Notes
An artist can operate as a seller, curator, content owner, and collaborator depending on the workflow.

### Verified Artist

#### Name
Verified Artist

#### Definition
An artist whose identity, portfolio, or legitimacy has been reviewed and approved by the platform.

#### Purpose
To increase buyer trust and distinguish recognized or approved producers in the marketplace.

#### Used By
Artist, Admin, Customer

#### Related Terms
Artist, Verification, Verification Request, Featured Artist

#### Database Impact
Likely linked to verification state, review history, and badge or indicator metadata.

#### API Impact
Verification status endpoints and public profile badge endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Badge display, verification announcement states, and trust messaging in product pages and profiles.

#### Business Rules
- Verification status must be authoritative and not inferred from user claims alone.
- Verified status may be used for trust signals but must not replace legal or operational safeguards.

#### Notes
Verified status is a trust signal, not necessarily a guarantee of total business risk elimination.

### Admin

#### Name
Admin

#### Definition
A platform-level operator responsible for governance, moderation, support, reports, settings, and operational oversight.

#### Purpose
To maintain trust, safety, and platform integrity at scale.

#### Used By
Admin

#### Related Terms
Moderator, Support Agent, CMS, Audit Log, System Settings, Moderation

#### Database Impact
Likely connected to governance settings, moderation queues, user roles, audit events, and platform settings.

#### API Impact
Admin dashboards, moderation, analytics, reporting, audit, and configuration endpoints.

#### UI Impact
Admin dashboard, moderation tools, analytics dashboards, and operational controls.

#### Business Rules
- Admins must not override documented business rules without controlled authority.
- Sensitive actions must be logged and reviewable.

#### Notes
Admin access is role-based and must be limited to explicitly permitted actions.

### Moderator

#### Name
Moderator

#### Definition
A user or operational role focused on reviewing reported, flagged, or questionable content and interactions.

#### Purpose
To protect marketplace quality, safety, and trust while reducing manual admin burden.

#### Used By

## 6. Glossary Governance

This glossary should be treated as the official vocabulary for the FANNAN specification set. Future documents must prefer the terms defined here and should avoid introducing synonyms without a deliberate governance decision.

When a new concept is introduced, the team should decide whether it is:

- a new official glossary term,
- a specialized internal term that should remain undocumented to end users, or

## 7. Summary
