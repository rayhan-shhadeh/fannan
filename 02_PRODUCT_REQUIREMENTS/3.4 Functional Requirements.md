## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the core functional requirements for FANNAN, a premium Palestinian digital marketplace for artists, artisans, buyers, and operational staff. It translates the product vision, role model, and user stories into testable system requirements for design, frontend, backend, mobile, QA, DevOps, and operations.

### 1.2 Scope

FANNAN shall support public discovery, authenticated customer activity, approved artist marketplace operations, governed commerce workflows, notifications, analytics, and internal administration. Requirements are organized by product module:

1. Authentication
2. Profiles
3. Marketplace
4. Products
5. Orders
6. Checkout
7. Payments
8. Shipping
9. Search
10. Notifications
11. Analytics
12. Admin
13. Artist Dashboard
14. Customer Dashboard

### 1.3 Conventions

The key words "shall", "shall not", "should", and "may" are used according to IEEE SRS convention. "Shall" defines a mandatory requirement. "Shall not" defines a prohibited behavior. "Should" defines a recommended behavior subject to product approval. "May" defines an optional behavior that requires its own documented scope before implementation.
