## 1. Overview

This document defines the features available to Artists and artisans using FANNAN. It covers the artist lifecycle from initial account creation and verification through public portfolio creation, catalogue management, commercial operations, financial settlement, communication, and performance insights.

The artist experience must remain artist-first, commercially usable, and trustworthy. Artists should be able to present work accurately, manage availability and sales, communicate with buyers, handle commissions, and understand performance while preserving the rights, context, and identity of their creative practice.

This document describes product behavior only. It does not define database fields, undocumented API endpoints, provider contracts, ranking algorithms, moderation policies, or payment settlement internals. Exact request and response contracts, persisted fields, and workflow states shall be documented in the relevant API, data model, commerce, moderation, and analytics specifications before implementation.

## 4. Artist Registration

### 4.1 Purpose

Artist registration lets a creator create a FANNAN account with the capabilities required to build a public portfolio, publish artwork, receive orders, request payouts, and manage commissions.

### 4.2 Workflow

1. User chooses to register as Artist.
2. System collects required identity and profile information in accordance with documented onboarding rules.
3. System creates the authenticated artist account and assigns the artist role and baseline permissions.
4. Artist continues to onboarding tasks such as verification, profile setup, and first artwork submission.
5. System surfaces clear next steps and indicates which actions remain incomplete.

### 4.3 Business Rules

- Registration shall be available only for eligible applicants under documented approval rules.
- The artist account shall permit marketplace and portfolio access only after identity or onboarding requirements are satisfied when required by policy.
- Registration shall not create public marketplace visibility until the artist is eligible and approved for public listing according to policy and review workflows.
- Unverified or restricted artists shall see clear status messaging and permitted actions without confusion.
- The registration process shall support Arabic and English and present RTL-friendly experience states.

### 4.4 Validation

- Required fields and consent requirements shall be validated before account creation.
- Account creation shall maintain auditability for onboarding decisions, role assignment, and any later status changes.
- Registration flows shall safely handle duplicate accounts, blocked identities, suspended users, and restricted regions.

### 4.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Start artist registration and complete eligible onboarding steps. |
| Artist | Manage own artist account and access artist dashboard capabilities once activated. |
| Admin | Review account creation outcomes, role assignment, and onboarding exceptions. |

### 4.6 Edge Cases

- If registration is interrupted, the system shall preserve draft data and resume safely.
- If an artist is later rejected or suspended, the platform shall remove or restrict public capabilities without deleting the account record.
- If identity evidence is incomplete, the artist shall remain on a guided pending state rather than being admitted with partial access.

### 4.7 Acceptance Criteria

- An eligible user can register as an artist from the public or mobile experience.
- The artist receives a clear onboarding path and status view.
- The account is not exposed as an active public selling account until all required eligibility checks are satisfied.

## 5. Verification

### 5.1 Purpose

Verification lets artists demonstrate authenticity, professionalism, and compliance with FANNAN trust requirements. Verified status increases confidence for buyers and supports premium marketplace presentation.

### 5.2 Workflow

1. Artist starts verification from the artist dashboard.
2. Artist submits required identity, practice, or business evidence as permitted by policy.
3. System stores the submission in a review queue and confirms the pending status.
4. Reviewer or admin validates the evidence against documented trust criteria.
5. System updates the artist status to verified, pending, rejected, or requires more information.
6. Artist sees the decision and any next steps required.

### 5.3 Business Rules

- Verification status shall be explicit and server-authoritative.
- Unverified artists may be able to access some profile features but shall not be treated as trusted sellers until approved.
- Verification shall not expose suppressed or private material beyond the data required for review.
- Verification outcomes must be auditable and explainable.
- Artists may resubmit or update evidence when requirements change.

### 5.4 Validation

- Verification requests shall validate completeness and required file or document format requirements.
- Reviewer actions shall be restricted to authorized operations.
- A rejected verification request shall preserve the history without revealing internal review commentary to the public.

### 5.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Submit or manage own verification evidence and view status. |
| Admin | Review, approve, reject, request more evidence, and manage verification policy. |
| Moderator | View eligible operational details when required for reviewing public trust concerns. |

### 5.6 Edge Cases

- If an artist submits expired or invalid evidence, the system shall request correction without destroying the attempt.
- If verification is revoked, the artist shall lose trusted presentation attributes while retaining account access as allowed by policy.
- If multiple review attempts are pending, the most recent valid review decision shall govern the active status.

### 5.7 Acceptance Criteria

- An artist can initiate verification and monitor status.
- Approved artists receive a visible trust indicator in permitted contexts.
- Rejected or incomplete verification clearly instructs the next action without exposing internal staff notes.

## 6. Portfolio

### 6.1 Purpose

The portfolio is the artist’s public identity page. It presents their practice, selected works, stories, and context in a way that helps buyers understand the artist beyond individual listings.

### 6.2 Workflow

1. Artist enters or edits portfolio information in the dashboard.
2. System provides sections for biography, specialties, story, featured works, and social proof where supported.
3. Artist chooses which works appear in the public portfolio.
4. Portfolio pages render to guests and customers according to eligibility rules.
5. Artist can organize preferred works into curated sections and highlight their public identity.

### 6.3 Business Rules

- Portfolio content shall be public only when the artist is eligible to appear publicly.
- The portfolio shall not reveal private contact details, private messages, or internal notes.
- Featured public works shall show accurate title, availability, and current status.
- Artists may hide or unpublish sections without deleting the underlying data.
- Portfolio discovery must remain aligned with marketplace search and browse rules.

### 6.4 Validation

- Portfolio sections shall validate required text, media constraints, and accessibility requirements.
- Public portfolio pages shall only surface eligible, approved content.
- Links and CTAs shall resolve to permitted destinations such as product pages, commission requests, or follow actions.

### 6.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Create, edit, and publish portfolio sections within own profile. |
| Guest | View eligible public portfolio pages. |
| Customer | View public portfolio pages and follow or contact through documented actions. |
| Admin | Review portfolio compliance and approve public presentation when required. |

### 6.6 Edge Cases

- If an artist removes a work from the marketplace, the portfolio shall reflect the current public status.
- If a portfolio item becomes suspended or rejected, it shall be omitted from public portfolio surfaces without deleting the artist record.
- If a language is unavailable, the system shall fall back to approved defaults while preserving directionality and clarity.

### 6.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can create a portfolio with profile information, story, and featured works.
- Public pages show these components only when the artist and works are eligible.
- Artists can update content without breaking existing product or commission links.

## 7. Artwork Upload

### 7.1 Purpose

Artwork upload allows artists to publish original work with complete, accurate presentation details needed for customer evaluation and purchase.

### 7.2 Workflow

1. Artist creates a new artwork record.
2. Artist uploads media files, previews, and alternate views where supported.
3. Artist enters required product content such as title, description, medium, dimensions, edition, and story context.
4. Artist adds tags, categories, and public visibility settings.
5. System validates completeness, media quality, and readiness for marketplace publication.
6. System moves the item into draft, pending review, or published status based on rules.

### 7.3 Business Rules

- Artwork shall require accurate representation and must not use misleading imagery or false claims.
- Media file handling shall meet documented performance, accessibility, and format constraints.
- A work may be saved as draft before full public publication.
- Publicly visible work must meet marketplace review standards and be consistent with eligibility rules.
- Missing information that is required for sale or discovery shall block publication until fixed.

### 7.4 Validation

- Media upload shall validate format, size, dimension, and quality constraints.
- Required title, description, and sale-information fields shall be validated before publication.
- Previews and alt text shall be available for accessibility and search clarity.

### 7.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Upload, edit, archive, and manage own artwork. |
| Admin | Review or intervene on restricted or flagged artwork. |
| Moderator | Review public compliance concerns and mark actionable content. |

### 7.6 Edge Cases

- If an upload fails mid-process, the system shall keep a recoverable draft state.
- If media is removed from storage, the system shall present a clean error and retain safe invalid-status handling.
- If artwork is later paused or rejected, the public page shall no longer imply active availability.

### 7.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can create and save a draft of new artwork.
- Required media and metadata are validated before listing goes live.
- Published artworks display correctly in marketplace, portfolio, and search contexts.

## 8. Inventory

### 8.1 Purpose

Inventory management allows artists to control the stock and status of each work they sell. It ensures that availability is accurate for customers and that sale signals remain trustworthy.

### 8.2 Workflow

1. Artist creates or edits a product listing.
2. Artist sets stock, availability mode, and item-level status.
3. Stock updates automatically with purchases or manual changes when permitted.
4. Artist may pause, restock, or archive inventory.
5. Public catalogue reflects the latest authoritative stock state.

### 8.3 Business Rules

- Inventory shall be server-authoritative and reflected in public listing states.
- Out-of-stock or unavailable items must not be presented as purchasable.
- Artwork may have multiple inventory states such as available, sold out, reserved, paused, or archived.
- Each inventory change must be visible in the artist dashboard and reflected accurately on public-facing product pages.

### 8.4 Validation

- Inventory values must remain realistic and use documented constraints.
- Automated updates from order events shall not exceed permissible stock or state transitions.
- Public listing pages shall only show items eligible for purchase.

### 8.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Manage inventory for their own works. |
| Admin | Review business exceptions and override only through documented approvals. |

### 8.6 Edge Cases

- If a sale occurs on a limited-edition work, the system shall prevent duplicate or erroneous sales.
- If an item is set to unavailable while pending orders exist, the platform shall follow documented order and fulfillment handling rules.
- If inventory is temporarily inconsistent due to sync delay, the system shall recover to authoritative state without exposing stale availability.

### 8.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can update stock and availability without direct customer-facing confusion.
- Sold-out or unavailable works are not sold through the marketplace.
- Public inventory states remain consistent with order fulfillment workflows.

## 9. Pricing

### 9.1 Purpose

Pricing tools allow artists to set and manage how much a work costs, including currency, pricing tiers, and sale condition rules.

### 9.2 Workflow

1. Artist enters product price and currency in the dashboard.
2. Artist configures sale orientation, such as standard price or limited-time offer if supported.
3. System validates the price against marketplace rules and currency constraints.
4. Product page presents the current price in a clear and localized format.
5. Artist may update pricing as conditions change.

### 9.3 Business Rules

- Pricing shall be visible only to the extent allowed by policy and the product state.
- The price shown to customers must match the authoritative sale price.
- Artists shall not be able to set prices that violate allowed pricing thresholds or marketplace rules without authorized exception handling.
- Price updates shall be reflected consistently across the product page, cart, checkout, and order flows.

### 9.4 Validation

- Currency and numeric formatting shall be validated for permitted values.
- Price-related actions shall consider taxes, fees, and shipping only where those flows are explicitly approved and documented.
- The system shall prevent invalid or empty pricing states for active products.

### 9.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Set pricing on own artworks and update price as conditions change. |
| Admin | Review pricing policy violations and approve exceptions if allowed. |

### 9.6 Edge Cases

- If an item is in draft or paused, hidden pricing details shall not appear in public discovery surfaces.
- If price changes occur after a cart is created, the platform shall use documented checkout rules to preserve buyer trust.
- If currency conversion or regional pricing is enabled, the system shall clearly disclose the rule and applicability.

### 9.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can assign and update a valid price for each product.
- Public price values are accurate and consistent across the purchase journey.
- Illegal or invalid price states are blocked or surfaced for correction.

## 10. Discounts

### 10.1 Purpose

Discounts help artists manage promotional offers without compromising marketplace trust. They support seasonal campaigns, studio promotions, and artist-led sales events.

### 10.2 Workflow

1. Artist creates a discount configuration for eligible products.
2. Artist defines the discount type, time window, target scope, and usage limitations.
3. System validates the discount against marketplace and product rules.
4. Eligible products display the discount in relevant public contexts.

### 10.3 Business Rules

- Discounts shall be explicit, transparent, and applied only to eligible products.
- Discount rules must be documented and enforceable server-side.
- Discount messages shall not imply active offers beyond the documented validity window.
- Artists shall not create deceptive scarcity or misleading promotional framing.

### 10.4 Validation

- Discount configurations must validate product eligibility, date range, and user conditions.
- Checkout shall only apply a valid, active discount to eligible purchases.
- Discount amounts shall remain visible and auditable in the order record.

### 10.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Create and manage own discounts where the feature is enabled. |
| Admin | Review policy compliance and manage global approval boundaries. |

### 10.6 Edge Cases

- If an offer expires, the platform shall remove the discount from active product presentations.
- If a product becomes ineligible, the discount shall not be applied at checkout.
- If a discount conflicts with an existing coupon or promotion, the system shall resolve according to documented precedence rules.

### 10.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can create valid discounts for eligible works.
- Discounts are applied only when conditions are met.
- Discounted prices remain truthful and clear in marketplace and order records.

## 11. Coupons

### 11.1 Purpose

Coupons support targeted artist or marketplace promotional campaigns and customer acquisition events while preserving clarity around who can use them and under what conditions.

### 11.2 Workflow

1. Artist or approved admin creates a coupon rule.
2. Artist defines the coupon scope, value, conditions, and expiry.
3. Customer or artist acting as customer enters or applies the coupon during purchase if eligible.
4. System validates the coupon against product, order, and user conditions.
5. Checkout shows the calculated price and the coupon outcome.

### 11.3 Business Rules

- Coupon eligibility must be explicit and enforceable.
- Coupon application shall be visible to customer and seller at order review time.
- Coupons shall not be applied retroactively after order submission without documented approval.
- Coupon logic shall avoid manipulation, stacking conflicts, or hidden eligibility exceptions.

### 11.4 Validation

- Code, scope, and expiration rules shall be validated before activation.
- System shall prevent invalid or expired coupon application at checkout.
- Order records shall retain the final applied coupon state for support and reconciliation.

### 11.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Create and manage coupons for own eligible catalogue where feature policy allows. |
| Admin | Establish boundary rules, review misuse, and manage escalation scenarios. |

### 11.6 Edge Cases

- If a coupon is removed after a cart is created, it shall follow the documented cart validity rules.
- If a product is archived or unavailable, the coupon shall not be applied to purchase it.
- If a coupon has limited redemption count, the checkout shall enforce the cap server-side.

### 11.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can create and manage valid coupons within policy limits.
- Customers can apply eligible coupons only when criteria are met.
- The final order reflects the coupon discount accurately and transparently.

### 12.4 Validation

## 13. Commissions

### 13.1 Purpose

Commissions allow customers to request custom work from artists. The feature provides a safe, structured form for description, budget, timeline, and expectations while keeping private communication and the final contract process under control.

### 13.2 Workflow

1. Customer opens a commission request flow for an artist.
2. Customer submits project details, desired scope, timeline, and budget intentions.
3. Artist receives a commission request in the dashboard.
4. Artist can accept, decline, revise, or counter the request.

### 13.3 Business Rules

- Artists must be able to accept, decline, or request clarification without exposing private contact details publicly.
- Commission status shall not be inferred from public portfolio listings alone.
- Customer and artist communication must remain within approved workflows unless separately documented.

### 13.4 Validation

- Request forms must validate required details and constraints before submission.
- The artist response must be recorded against the correct request and customer.
- Accepted or completed commissions must reflect final scope, pricing, and delivery expectations.

### 13.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |

| Customer | Create, view, and manage their own commission requests. |
| Admin | Review escalations, compliance issues, and operational status where needed. |

### 13.6 Edge Cases

- If a commission request is submitted without required details, the artist shall see a request in an incomplete or pending state until clarified.
- If an artist declines, the customer shall receive a clear refusals or next-step message without public disclosure.
- If the scope changes after acceptance, the system shall capture the revision before fulfillment continues.

### 13.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can receive and manage commission requests in a controlled workflow.
- Customers can submit requests with structured details and track their status.
- Commission decisions and communication remain clear, auditable, and limited to authorized participants.

## 14. Followers

- Artists shall only see follower information in permitted contexts and within privacy-safe boundaries.
- Follow actions shall not expose private customer data or trigger unintended contact.

### 14.4 Validation

- Follow and unfollow actions must verify the user and the target artist before updating state.
- Public follower counts must reflect current, server-authoritative values.
- The system shall prevent duplicate follow state and stale counts.

### 14.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |

### 14.6 Edge Cases

### 14.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artist-facing follower data reflects real current state and respects privacy rules.
- Follow actions do not trigger unsupported access or contact behavior.

### 17.4 Validation

- Final payout state shall remain auditable and explainable.

### 17.5 Permissions

| Admin | Review payout exceptions and account restrictions. |

### 17.6 Edge Cases

- If the artist lacks a valid payout method, the platform shall present a clear next step rather than allowing a failed request.
- If a payout is placed on hold due to compliance or account review, the artist shall see the status and required action.
- If the payout account changes, the system shall require secure proof or validation if policy requires it.

### 17.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can request payout of eligible funds with clear status tracking.
- The system prevents invalid payout requests and accurately records successful withdrawal outcomes.
- Payout status remains understandable and auditable.

- Public and internal settings shall be separated clearly.

### 18.4 Validation

### 18.5 Permissions

### 18.6 Edge Cases

### 18.7 Acceptance Criteria

Portfolio story gives artists a place to explain their practice, cultural context, themes, creative process, materials, and influences. It helps buyers understand the work beyond product metadata.

### 20.4 Validation

## 21. Media Management

### 21.1 Purpose

Media management lets artists organize and maintain their visual assets, previews, and supporting files in a controlled way across profile, portfolio, and product listings.

### 21.2 Workflow

1. Artist uploads or selects media assets for use in artwork and profile sections.
2. Artist organizes assets by type, product, or collection.
3. System retains approved versions, previews, and fallback states.
4. Artist replaces, removes, or archives media assets as the catalogue changes.
5. Public surfaces use only the current approved asset version.

### 21.3 Business Rules

- Media management must support safe, consistent reuse without duplicating or mixing asset versions incorrectly.
- Media assets shall respect format, size, quality, and accessibility rules.
- Public surfaces may only use approved media assets aligned with the product state.
- Draft, rejected, or archived media shall not be displayed in public catalogues.

### 21.4 Validation

- Asset uploads shall validate supported file types, size limits, and compressed output rules.
- Media metadata such as alt text and descriptions shall be included where required.
- Asset replacement shall retain the correct public association and avoid orphaned references.

### 21.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |

| Admin | Review compliance and manage asset restrictions or removal. |
| Moderator | Review flagged or non-compliant media content when needed. |

### 21.6 Edge Cases

- If a file is corrupted or processing fails, the artist shall retain a safe status and a retry path.
- If media is removed from a live product, the product page shall degrade gracefully without exposing broken references.
- If multiple versions exist, the platform shall reference only the current approved media asset.

### 21.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Artists can manage their media library for profile and artwork content.
- Only valid assets are used in the public catalogue and portfolio.
- Asset transitions remain safe and understandable during updates, removals, or replacements.

## 22. Cross-Feature Requirements

### 22.1 Authentication and Authorization

All artist features must be enforced server-side by the documented REST API. Client interfaces may present controls and page states, but they must never be treated as the source of truth for authorization.

### 22.2 Accessibility and Localization

Artist features shall support Arabic and English, preserve RTL/LTR flow, use meaningful focus states, and maintain readable contrast and semantics across web and mobile. Media, forms, and status indicators shall be understandable without relying on color alone.

### 22.3 Trust and Safety

The product shall provide clear status states for draft, pending review, approved, rejected, paused, sold out, archived, and payment or compliance holds. Artists must have clear next steps when a feature or listing is restricted, incomplete, or requires attention.

### 22.4 Operational Visibility

Artist features shall maintain a predictable, auditable, and supportable record of state changes for reviews, payouts, orders, commissions, and content decisions. This is essential for trust, dispute resolution, and SLA-driven support workflows.

## 23. Scope Boundaries

### 23.1 In Scope

- Artist registration and onboarding
- Verification and trust indicators
- Portfolio and story management
- Artwork creation, upload, and publishing
- Inventory and pricing management
- Discount, coupon, and order workflows
- Commission requests and communication
- Follower and analytics visibility
- Withdrawal and account settings
- Media management and profile customization

### 23.2 Out of Scope

- Unapproved social feed features beyond the documented marketplace workflows
- Direct-message features outside approved order and commission threads
- Auctions, resale, subscriptions, and NFT workflows
- AI-generated recommendations or fabricated trend claims
- Unapproved external payout networks or unsupported financial rails

## 24. Summary

The artist features defined in this specification establish the core capabilities required for Palestinian artists and artisans to present work, manage a catalogue, sell with confidence, build a customer base, and operate a professional creative business through FANNAN. These features are organized around trust, commercial clarity, accessibility, and controlled access to both public and private workflows.

The implementation of these features must follow the product’s documented architecture, role boundaries, and API conventions. Any database fields, endpoints, business rules, or payment and moderation flows not explicitly approved in the relevant specifications shall not be invented or implemented ad hoc.
