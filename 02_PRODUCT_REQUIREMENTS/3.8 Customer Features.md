## 1. Overview

This document defines the customer-facing functionality available to FANNAN customers. It covers the full buyer lifecycle from account creation and authentication through discovery, product evaluation, cart and checkout, order tracking, saved items, artist follow relationships, commission requests, and personal account management.

The customer journey must feel clear, trusted, and premium while supporting a culturally grounded marketplace. Customers should be able to browse authentic work, evaluate artist practice confidently, buy with clarity, save items for later, communicate with sellers when appropriate, and manage their account and order history without friction.

This document describes product behavior only. It does not define database fields, undocumented API endpoints, payment-provider contracts, shipping provider contracts, ranking algorithms, moderation policies, or delivery internals. Exact request and response shapes, persisted fields, analytics events, state machines, and operational rules shall be documented in the relevant API, data model, commerce, checkout, payments, shipping, notification, and analytics specifications before implementation.

## 4. Registration

### 4.1 Purpose

Customer registration allows a new buyer to create a FANNAN account, establish access to marketplace features, and begin the trust-building journey required to save, follow, purchase, and request commissions.

### 4.2 Workflow

1. Visitor chooses to create a customer account.
2. System collects required profile and account data within documented validation rules.
3. System creates the customer account and assigns the baseline customer role.
4. Customer completes account setup, preferences, and any verification or onboarding steps that are required for trust-sensitive actions.
5. System surfaces the next actions available to the customer after successful registration.

### 4.3 Business Rules

- Registration shall be available only to eligible users under documented approval and policy rules.
- Customers shall not be able to create an account that grants unauthorized artist or admin privileges.
- Public buyer profile creation shall not expose internal or operational staff metadata.
- The registration flow shall support Arabic and English and remain usable in RTL and LTR layouts.
- Registration shall preserve safe intent when the user is attempting to complete an account-required action before signing in.

### 4.4 Validation

- Required fields, consent, and account uniqueness checks shall be validated before account creation.
- Duplicate account, blocked account, and restricted-region cases shall be handled with clear messaging.
- Role assignment and access state shall be created server-side and not inferred from client-side UI state.

### 4.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Begin registration and complete the account-creation flow. |
| Customer | Manage own account, profile, settings, and customer-only actions. |
| Admin | Review registration outcomes and account exceptions when required by operational policy. |

### 4.6 Edge Cases

- If registration is interrupted, the system shall retain the partially completed form and resume safely.
- If a user attempts to create an account with an existing identity or blocked status, the system shall reject the action with a clear explanation.
- If onboarding steps remain incomplete, the account shall remain usable only within the permitted scope until the missing requirements are resolved.

### 4.7 Acceptance Criteria

- An eligible visitor can create a customer account from the public web or mobile experience.
- The system creates the customer role and provides the next steps for buyer journey actions.
- Registration is rejected or redirected appropriately for blocked, duplicate, or ineligible cases.

## 5. Authentication

### 5.1 Purpose

Authentication allows customers to access protected marketplace actions, personal account data, saved items, order history, addresses, and communication with artists while maintaining secure access control.

### 5.2 Workflow

1. Customer enters account credentials or continues through the approved account sign-in flow.
2. System verifies identity against the documented authentication rules.
3. System establishes an authenticated session or token with the correct customer scope.
4. Customer is returned to their intended destination or the appropriate dashboard area.
5. System keeps the session secure and re-authenticates where policy requires it.

### 5.3 Business Rules

- The server shall enforce authorization on every protected route and operation.
- Customer actions shall never rely on client-only control visibility to grant access.
- Sign-in shall preserve safe re-entry for products, carts, saved items, and order states after authentication.
- Authenticated customers shall be able to complete only the actions permitted by their role and policy.

### 5.4 Validation

- Credentials, MFA or second-factor requirements, session validity, and timeout behavior shall be validated according to policy.
- Failed sign-in attempts shall be handled securely without exposing account existence when not allowed by policy.
- Session expiry and refresh actions shall maintain uninterrupted access only within documented rules.

### 5.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Access public marketplace content and start sign-in or sign-up flows. |
| Customer | Sign in, view private account data, complete buyer actions, and manage own order and profile data. |
| Admin | Review operational account security events and support cases when necessary. |

### 5.6 Edge Cases

- If the customer session expires, the system shall preserve the user’s intended path or redirect to a safe sign-in state.
- If authentication fails repeatedly, the system shall enforce safe lockout and recovery steps according to policy.
- If the user signs out on one device, only the current device’s session state shall be invalidated according to the documented session model.

### 5.7 Acceptance Criteria

- A registered customer can sign in and access their buyer features securely.
- Authentication preserves the user’s valid intended destination after sign-in.
- Protected actions remain inaccessible without valid credentials and server-side authorization.

## 6. Search

### 6.1 Purpose

Search helps customers find artworks, artists, collections, and categories using product and contextual discovery patterns aligned with buyer intent.

### 6.2 Workflow

1. Customer enters a query from homepage, catalog page, or navigation entry.
2. System matches the request against approved public catalogue content and artist metadata.
3. System returns relevant results and supports refinement through filters or sorting where available.
4. Customer opens a product or artist page from search results.
5. System preserves search context when navigating into deeper marketplace discovery.

### 6.3 Business Rules

- Search results shall only include eligible public content and approved catalogue items.
- Search performance, empty states, and relevance behavior shall meet accessibility and localization requirements.
- Search results shall not expose internal draft, suspended, or restricted items.
- Search filters and sorting shall be clear, truthful, and consistent with documented marketplace rules.

### 6.4 Validation

- Query handling shall validate input length, acceptable characters, and safe processing rules.
- Search results shall be derived from server-authoritative catalogue rules rather than highly client-side assumptions.
- Empty or no-result states shall guide the user toward alternative discovery paths.

### 6.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Search public catalogue and public artist pages. |
| Customer | Search and access the full public catalogue plus authenticated activity states. |
| Artist | Search public marketplace content for discovery and comparison. |
| Admin | Review search configuration and operational results when needed. |

### 6.6 Edge Cases

- If the query produces no results, the system shall show a safe empty state with guidance rather than an empty screen.
- If a matching item becomes unavailable after search, the product page shall reflect the current state.
- If the user searches in Arabic or mixed-script content, search and result presentation shall respect language behavior and RTL/LTR handling.

### 6.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can search for artwork and artist content using approved marketplace indexes.
- Results are limited to eligible public items and are presented with clear metadata.
- Search remains usable across web and mobile and respects accessibility requirements.

- Browse sections shall only present eligible public content and approved editorial groupings.
- Featured and curated sections shall avoid fabricated claims, misleading urgency, or undisclosed sponsored placement.
- Empty sections shall show approved empty states rather than suggesting fabricated inventory.
- Browse navigation shall remain accessible and understandable in both Arabic and English.

### 7.4 Validation

- Categories and collections shall validate active status and public visibility before rendering.
- Browse modules shall handle missing content without breaking the page or presenting stale items.
- Product and artist groupings shall remain consistent with server-authoritative product states.

### 7.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | View public browse sections and collection entry points. |
| Customer | View browse sections and perform eligible authenticated actions such as save or follow. |
| Artist | View public browse surfaces for discovery and market awareness. |
| Admin | Configure and manage browse rules and editorial groupings. |

### 7.6 Edge Cases

- If a curated collection is empty or temporarily disabled, the UI shall render a useful empty-state rather than a broken layout.
- If an item moves from featured to unavailable, its display state shall be refreshed at the next authoritative data load.
- If browse categories are localized, the system shall preserve content translation and layout direction accurately.

### 7.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Collections do not include hidden or ineligible items.
- Navigation is clear, accessible, and consistent across device sizes.

## 8. Product Details

### 8.1 Purpose

Product details provide customers with the relevant information needed to evaluate a work before purchase, including visuals, context, pricing, availability, artist information, and next actions.

### 8.2 Workflow

1. Customer opens a product page from search, browse, collection, or direct link.

3. Customer evaluates the work and may choose actions such as save, follow the artist, learn more, or add to cart.

### 8.3 Business Rules

- Product pages shall display only current, eligible public data.
- Pricing, availability, materials, dimensions, and edition details shall be accurate and consistent with the documented marketplace rules.
- Artist identity and the work’s context shall remain visible to support trust and provenance.
- Customer actions shall align with the product’s current eligibility and stock state.

### 8.4 Validation

- Product metadata shall be validated for presence, format, and consistency before public display.
- Product pages shall handle unavailable, sold-out, archived, or restricted work with safe fallback states.
- Image alt text and descriptive content shall support accessibility and localization.

### 8.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | View eligible public product pages. |
| Customer | View public product pages and perform eligible authenticated buyer actions. |
| Artist | View product pages as a marketplace participant and manage own listings. |
| Admin | Review product compliance and operational issues when needed. |

### 8.6 Edge Cases

- If a product is sold out after the page loads, the page shall reflect the latest server state before the customer attempts purchase.
- If a product is temporarily paused, the UI shall avoid implying active availability.
- If a product has multiple media variants, the page shall present a safe fallback if one image fails to load.

### 8.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can view product information, artist context, and current pricing and availability from one page.
- Product pages prevent false or misleading public claims.
- Actions available on the page match the current product eligibility at the time of interaction.

## 9. Shopping Cart

### 9.1 Purpose

The shopping cart lets customers assemble eligible items, review totals, and prepare to complete a purchase with the relevant price, item, and quantity information.

### 9.2 Workflow

1. Customer adds eligible product items to the cart.
2. System records the cart contents and updates totals in real time or per the documented refresh rules.
3. Customer reviews item quantity, price, eligibility, and order summary.
4. Customer proceeds to checkout or updates the cart before completion.
5. System prevents invalid or stale cart operations when products become unavailable or prices change.

### 9.3 Business Rules

- Cart contents shall be derived from documented product and inventory rules.
- Customers shall not add unavailable, expired, or ineligible items to cart if the system forbids them.
- Price and product state shall remain truthful when the cart is reviewed or checked out.
- Cart state shall be recoverable and consistent across device sessions where supported by policy.

### 9.4 Validation

- Quantity validation, stock checks, and product eligibility checks shall happen before a cart action is accepted.
- Cart totals and item summaries shall be refreshed if a product or price changes.
- Stale or invalid cart entries shall be removed or flagged with the proper user guidance.

### 9.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Add, remove, update, and review own cart items. |
| Artist acting as Customer | Manage their own cart within the customer role. |
| Admin | Review operational exceptions and support issues. |

### 9.6 Edge Cases

- If a cart item becomes unavailable, the buyer shall be notified and the item shall be blocked or removed according to policy.
- If a price changes after item addition, the platform shall resolve the price according to documented cart validity rules.
- If a customer attempts to exceed purchase limits, the system shall reject the action with a clear explanation.

### 9.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can add eligible items to cart and update quantities.
- Cart pricing and availability remain current and truthful.
- Invalid cart state is prevented or repaired with clear user feedback.

## 11. Checkout

### 11.1 Purpose

Checkout allows customers to confirm their order, provide required buyer information, and complete the purchase in a structured and trustworthy flow.

### 11.2 Workflow

1. Customer enters checkout from a valid cart state.
2. System verifies cart validity, product availability, and current pricing.
3. Customer reviews order summary, selected delivery or collection options, and payment information as approved.
4. System confirms the order after validation and creates the official order record.
5. Customer receives the order confirmation and relevant next-step communication.

### 11.3 Business Rules

- Checkout shall require valid cart content, eligible product state, and documented customer data requirements.
- Customers shall not be able to complete checkout for unavailable or invalid products.
- Price, order totals, and policy disclosures shall be clear and consistent before submission.
- Payment, shipping, and order status terms shall align with approved operational rules and legal disclosures.

### 11.4 Validation

- Validity checks shall include product availability, pricing, inventory, addresses, payment method eligibility, and required customer inputs.
- Checkout shall reject invalid or incomplete orders before submission.
- Final order state shall be recorded server-side and shall not depend on the client’s local view.

### 11.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Complete checkout for their own valid cart and review order confirmation. |
| Admin | Review operational escalations and support issues related to checkout failures or disputes. |

### 11.6 Edge Cases

- If payment fails, the system shall preserve a recoverable checkout state and direct the customer back to the next correct action.
- If an item becomes unavailable during checkout, the order shall be stopped or re-priced according to documented rules.
- If a checkout is interrupted, the system shall keep the cart or order intent recoverable without exposing private customer data.

### 11.7 Acceptance Criteria

- A valid customer can complete checkout for eligible items.
- Purchases are blocked if the items, prices, or buyer criteria are invalid.
- Order confirmation is clear, accurate, and consistent with the final order state.

- Address formats and required fields must be validated before save or checkout use.
- Completion of shipping details must be required only when necessary for the order path.
- The checkout flow shall reject invalid address data or require correction.

### 17.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Manage own saved addresses and use them for valid orders. |
| Admin | Review operational exceptions and support needs. |
| Support Agent | Access case-specific addresses only under documented support rules. |

### 17.6 Edge Cases

- If a customer saves an incomplete address, the system shall not allow it to be used for shipping without correction.
- If a shipping address is changed after order creation, the system shall follow documented order-update rules.
- If a saved address becomes invalid or no longer supported, the customer shall be informed and directed to update it.

### 17.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can create, save, edit, and select valid shipping or billing addresses.
- Address data is used only in the supported order and account workflows.
- Invalid data is blocked from order execution.

## 18. Notifications

### 18.1 Purpose

Notifications keep customers informed about order updates, account activity, wishlist and follow updates, commission movement, and relevant marketplace events without requiring repeated manual checking.

### 18.2 Workflow

1. Customer receives an event from a supported workflow such as order confirmation, status change, commission response, or security-related update.
2. System creates or updates the notification record.
3. Customer sees the alert in dashboard, account panel, in-app notification area, or email/push channel where supported.
4. Customer can open, acknowledge, or manage notification preferences as allowed.

### 18.3 Business Rules

- Notifications shall be restricted to relevant customer events and permitted communication channels.
- Notification content shall remain truthful, concise, and consistent with the order, account, or commission state.
- Customers shall be able to control notification preferences in the account settings workflow.
- Notification delivery shall not be used to create spam, false urgency, or manipulative language.

### 18.4 Validation

- Notification triggers shall be based on permitted system events and correct customer ownership.
- Expired, unread, or duplicate notifications shall be handled by approved deduplication and display rules.
- Notification preferences shall validate supported channels and settings before save.

### 18.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View, manage, and configure own notifications. |
| Artist | Receive relevant notifications for order or commission activity on their own work. |
| Admin | Review operational notification issues and system health where required. |

### 18.6 Edge Cases

- If a customer disables a notification channel, the platform shall honor the preference and not send messages through that route.
- If an event is generated for a closed or invalid order, the system shall suppress or redirect it according to policy.
- If a notification cannot be delivered, the system shall log or surface the failure in a controlled way without exposing the entire event chain.

### 18.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers receive relevant notifications in the supported channels for their own actions and account events.
- Preferences can be adjusted without affecting unrelated account workflows.
- Notifications remain truthful and scoped to valid customer events.

## 19. Profile

### 19.1 Purpose

The customer profile provides a place to manage identity details, account preferences, saved information, and supported personal presentation without exposing sensitive operational data.

### 19.2 Workflow

1. Customer opens the profile section in the dashboard or account setup area.
2. Customer updates supported personal information and preferences.
3. System validates updates and stores the new profile settings.
4. Customer can review saved profile details and account state.

### 19.3 Business Rules

- Customer profile data shall be stored and managed under documented account rules and privacy boundaries.
- Public profile visibility shall be separated from private operational details and account data.
- Customers shall have appropriate control over their profile settings but not over business or moderation operations unrelated to their account.

### 19.4 Validation

- Profile fields shall validate format, completeness, and supported values.
- Security-sensitive profile changes shall follow the documented confirmation and authorization model.
- Public display fields shall avoid misleading or false claims about artist identity or account status.

### 19.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Manage own profile and account preferences within supported fields. |
| Admin | Review support or compliance issues that affect customer account management. |

### 19.6 Edge Cases

- If profile changes are interrupted, the system shall preserve a recoverable previous state or draft without exposing partial data.
- If account status changes due to suspension or restriction, the profile may become read-only or limited under policy.
- If a customer uses mixed-language values, the system shall preserve readability and directionality.

### 19.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can access and update the relevant parts of their profile.
- Profile updates are safe, validated, and consistent with account and privacy rules.
- The profile page does not expose data unrelated to the customer’s own account.

## 20. Order History

### 20.1 Purpose

Order history gives customers a clear view of past purchases, order status history, and relevant order lifecycle information needed for record-keeping and support.

### 20.2 Workflow

1. Customer opens the order history view.
2. System fetches approved orders associated with the customer account.
3. Customer filters, browses, or opens each order record as needed.
4. Customer can use the order history to support future purchases, customer support, or return or refund follow-up where allowed.

### 20.3 Business Rules

- Order history shall reflect only the customer’s own historical orders and approved support data.
- Historical order states must remain consistent with the documented order lifecycle and final order outcome.
- The customer shall not see information from other customers or orders outside their own scope.

### 20.4 Validation

- Historical order records must be tied to the correct customer and valid order ownership.
- Old order records shall remain readable and supportable within the documented retention model.
- Empty, archived, or cancelled orders shall be shown in a safe and accurate historical state.

### 20.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View and manage their own order history. |
| Admin | Review escalations and support workflows when required. |
| Support Agent | Access case-related history only under approved support rules. |

### 20.6 Edge Cases

- If a historical order has been cancelled or refunded, the record shall reflect the final state rather than only the previous pending status.
- If a customer account is restricted or closed, the history may be limited or available only under documented support rules.
- If a product is removed from the catalogue but the order still exists, the order history shall preserve the original transaction record without implying active availability.

### 20.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can review prior orders and their final or current state with confidence.
- Order history is complete, relevant, and restricted to the customer’s own account.
- Past purchases remain accessible without exposing private or unrelated data.

## 21. Cross-Feature Requirements

### 21.1 Authentication and Authorization

All customer features must be enforced server-side according to the documented REST API and role model. Client interfaces may render controls and states, but they must never be treated as the source of truth for access or entitlement.

### 21.2 Accessibility and Localization

Customer features must support Arabic and English, preserve correct reading direction, maintain clear focus states, and ensure accessibility across viewports starting from mobile widths. Interactive elements, status indications, and priority actions must remain understandable without relying on color alone.

### 21.3 Trust and Safety

The customer experience must communicate product, order, and account states clearly. The system must provide honest, non-manipulative signals for pricing, stock, availability, seller credibility, and support states. Urgency, scarcity, and promotional claims shall remain truthful.

### 21.4 Operational Visibility and Support

The customer experience must preserve a usable audit trail for orders, commission requests, account changes, and service workflows. This supports customer support, dispute resolution, moderation handling, and secure account administration.

## 22. Scope Boundaries

### 22.1 In Scope

- Customer registration and authentication
- Search, browse, and product discovery
- Product details, wishlist, favorites, and follow workflows
- Cart, checkout, orders, order history, and reviews
- Commission requests and address management
- Notification preferences and customer profile management

### 22.2 Out of Scope

- Public social feeds beyond approved marketplace interactions
- Direct message systems outside the documented order and commission workflows
- Auctions, resale, subscriptions, NFT sales, and unapproved digital collectibles
- AI-generated recommendation systems or fabricated trend claims
- Unapproved external payment or fulfillment flows not defined in the platform’s product specifications

## 23. Summary

The customer features defined in this specification establish the core buyer experience required for FANNAN: trust, product discovery, confident purchasing, account management, order visibility, and ongoing relationship-building with artists. These capabilities are designed to support a premium, culturally grounded marketplace while preserving clear boundaries between public product data, private account data, and operational staff workflows.

The implementation of these features must follow the product’s documented architecture, role model, and API conventions. Any database fields, endpoints, business rules, or operational flows not explicitly approved in the relevant specifications shall not be invented or implemented ad hoc.
