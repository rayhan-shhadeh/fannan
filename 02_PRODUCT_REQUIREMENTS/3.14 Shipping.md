## 1. Overview

This document defines the shipping experience for FANNAN. It covers shipping providers, rate calculation, shipping zones, delivery estimates, tracking, order status, returns, exchanges, packaging, customer notifications, and the future international shipping capability.

Shipping must be clear, predictable, and trustworthy for customers while remaining operationally feasible for artists and fulfillment teams. Customers must understand delivery costs, timing, and order progress before and after purchase, and operations teams must have a consistent process for fulfillment, exceptions, and updates.

This document describes product behavior only. It does not define database fields, undocumented API endpoints, shipping provider API contracts, carrier integration details, or internal implementation structure. Exact technical contracts, fulfillment rules, rate logic, and shipping telemetry shall be specified in downstream operational and API documents before implementation.

## 4. Shipping Providers

### 4.1 Purpose

Shipping providers define the delivery options available to customers for approved orders. The marketplace shall only use shipping methods that are valid for the destination, product type, fulfillment model, and operational ability.

### 4.2 Workflow

1. Customer selects a shipping destination in checkout.
2. System checks the current order against available shipping providers and service types.
3. System presents the valid shipping options with cost, timing, and service conditions.
4. Customer chooses a provider or the system applies the default valid option when policy allows.
5. Order and fulfillment records reflect the chosen shipping provider.

### 4.3 Business Rules

- Shipping providers must be approved for the market, destination, and product category.
- Providers must be hidden or disabled when not available for a selected order or destination.
- Customers must not be offered a provider that is not valid for their address, cart contents, or order value thresholds.
- Provider selection must remain consistent across the order summary, fulfillment steps, and tracking updates.

### 4.4 Validation

- Provider availability shall be checked against the selected region, shipping zone, product type, and fulfillment capability.
- Invalid provider combinations must block checkout or re-route the user to a valid option.
- Fulfillment or support exceptions must be clearly surfaced when a provider is unavailable or delayed.

### 4.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Select a valid shipping provider for their order. |
| Admin | Configure and manage available shipping providers and coverage rules. |
| Artist | View fulfilled shipping status related to their products and delivery obligations. |

### 4.6 Edge Cases

- If the destination is not covered by a provider, the system shall display the reason and offer a valid alternative or block the purchase.
- If a provider is temporarily unavailable, the system shall not silently default the customer to an invalid option.
- If the customer updates the address after provider selection, the system shall revalidate the provider and service options.

### 4.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Only valid shipping providers are shown for a given order and destination.
- Provider selection remains consistent across checkout, order status, and tracking.
- Customers are clearly informed when shipping is unavailable or restricted.

## 5. Shipping Rates

### 5.1 Purpose

Shipping rates determine the total delivery cost shown to the customer. Rates must be fair, understandable, and consistent with the selected shipping method and order conditions.

### 5.2 Workflow

1. Customer reviews shipping options.
2. System calculates rate based on destination, order value, product characteristics, and selected shipping method.
3. Customer sees rate and estimated delivery timing before payment confirmation.
4. Final order summary reflects the approved rate and any shipping adjustments.

### 5.3 Business Rules

- Shipping rates shall be calculated only from approved rules and valid service selections.
- Rate estimates shall be shown before payment confirmation and remain consistent with the final order summary.
- Free shipping or promotional rate policies must be clearly distinguished from standard rates.
- Price changes caused by address or product changes must be communicated before final order submission.

### 5.4 Validation

- Shipping rates must be recalculated whenever the order, destination, or selected service changes.
- A customer must not be charged for a shipping option that is invalid or no longer applicable.
- Rate differences due to promos, thresholds, or exceptions must remain clear and auditable.

### 5.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View shipping rates before confirmation. |
| Admin | Configure rate rules, thresholds, and exceptions. |
| Artist | View the shipping cost and fulfillment assumptions relevant to their items where approved. |

### 5.6 Edge Cases

- If a shipping rate is no longer valid after checkout updates, the system shall recalculate and show the new total before payment.
- If a carrier or provider returns a temporary failure when determining a rate, the system shall avoid a silent fallback to an incorrect value.
- If a promo or free-shipping threshold is crossed or not met, the system shall update the displayed total clearly.

### 5.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Recalculated rates remain accurate and match the selected shipping service and order conditions.
- Customers can understand the shipping cost before payment is submitted.
- Rate changes are reflected consistently in checkout and final order details.

## 6. Zones

### 6.1 Purpose

Shipping zones define geographic or delivery-area groupings used to apply provider coverage, rate rules, and fulfillment policy. Zones help explain why some destinations have different shipping options or costs.

### 6.2 Workflow

1. Customer enters or selects a shipping destination.
2. System maps the destination to the applicable shipping zone.
3. System applies corresponding rate and provider rules and presents the resulting options.
4. If the destination is unsupported for the selected order, the system blocks checkout or asks for a valid alternative.

### 6.3 Business Rules

- Every supported shipping destination must map to a valid shipping zone or be rejected.
- Zone rules shall be consistent with provider coverage and regional fulfillment policy.
- A customer shall not be presented with a shipping option that conflicts with the zone assignment.
- Zone logic must remain stable while still allowing operational updates by policy.

### 6.4 Validation

- Destination mapping must be checked before shipping rates are finalized.
- Unsupported destinations shall trigger a clear explanation and safe recovery path.
- Updates to regions or coverage must not leave stale zone assignments in active orders.

### 6.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | See valid zone-based shipping options for their destination. |
| Admin | Adjust and review shipping zone coverage and policies. |
| Artist | Understand shipping coverage for products sold in their assigned regions. |

### 6.6 Edge Cases

- If the destination changes after the zone was established, the system must re-evaluate the zone and rates.
- If a region is temporarily unavailable, the system shall show the restriction rather than silently skipping it.
- If a destination falls into a special zone requiring exception handling, the order must remain in a valid safe state.

### 6.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Shipping options are always aligned with the destination’s shipping zone.
- Unsupported destinations are clearly rejected or rerouted.
- Zone-based shipping logic remains consistent through the order lifecycle.

## 7. Delivery Estimates

### 7.1 Purpose

Delivery estimates provide customers with a realistic expected arrival window for their order. They must be transparent, aligned with the provider and service, and updated as the order progresses.

### 7.2 Workflow

1. System calculates available delivery estimates based on shipping method and selected destination.
2. Customer sees the estimated arrival window before payment confirmation.
3. After order placement, the system refines or preserves the estimate as fulfillment and shipping progress updates.
4. Customer receives status updates when the arrival estimate changes significantly.

### 7.3 Business Rules

- Delivery estimates must be based on approved provider timelines and operational constraints.
- The displayed estimate must be consistent with the selected shipping service and current fulfillment stage.
- Customers must be informed when a delivery window changes materially.
- Estimates must not promise a delivery date that is not supported by the shipping provider or operational policy.

### 7.4 Validation

- Estimated delivery windows shall be checked against the chosen provider and shipping zone.
- Order status updates must not conflict with previously communicated estimates without a valid reason.
- Delayed shipping or exception scenarios must trigger clear communication and support routing.

### 7.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View the delivery estimate for their orders. |
| Admin | Review carrier estimate accuracy and operational issues. |
| Artist | View the fulfillment timeline associated with their items when relevant. |

### 7.6 Edge Cases

- If a carrier delay occurs after order placement, the estimate must be updated with an explanation where appropriate.
- If an item is not eligible for a delivery method, the system must show the reason and prevent invalid selection.
- If a shipment is split or delayed, the order must retain a clear state that reflects the actual fulfillment situation.

### 7.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can understand expected delivery timing before purchase and after order placement.
- Delivery estimates remain valid and consistent with the current shipping provider and order state.
- Material delays or changes to expected delivery windows are communicated clearly.

## 8. Tracking

### 8.1 Purpose

Tracking gives customers visibility into the movement of their order after it is handed off to a shipping provider or fulfillment operation. It supports confidence, transparency, and support escalation.

### 8.2 Workflow

1. Order is packed or handed off to the shipping provider.
2. System assigns or receives the tracking reference associated with the shipment.
3. Customer can view the current shipment status and recent movement updates.
4. Support teams can review the tracking history when the customer asks for help.

### 8.3 Business Rules

- Tracking information must be available for eligible shipments and clearly labeled as unavailable when not applicable.
- Tracking updates must be consistent with the order status and shipping provider updates.
- Customers shall be able to distinguish between shipped, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, and delivery issues.
- Tracking data must not be exposed in a way that creates false certainty when the carrier has not confirmed the latest movement.

### 8.4 Validation

- Tracking data must align with shipment state and carrier or fulfillment updates.
- Missing, delayed, or stale tracking information must be surfaced as a valid order state rather than as a silent success.
- Customer-facing tracking summary must avoid exposing unsupported or unverified provider details.

### 8.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View order tracking details for their own shipment when available. |
| Admin | Review shipment tracking details and support exceptions. |
| Artist | View relevant shipment tracking status tied to their sold items when policy allows. |

### 8.6 Edge Cases

- If tracking is delayed or not yet assigned, the system shall clearly communicate that shipping is in progress but tracking is pending.
- If the shipment is undeliverable or returned to sender, the system shall create a clear exception state with next steps.
- If the package is split across multiple carriers, the order shall maintain a coherent overall status.

### 8.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can see whether their shipment has been tracked and its current movement status.
- Tracking and order status remain aligned and understandable.
- Delivery or shipment exceptions are surfaced clearly and recoverably.

## 9. Order Status

### 9.1 Purpose

Order status shows the customer and support teams the current state of an order in the fulfillment lifecycle. It must be legible, consistent, and actionable.

### 9.2 Workflow

1. Order is created and enters the created or confirmed state.
2. Order progresses through validation, packing, shipment, in-transit, delivery, and completion stages.
3. Status changes are displayed in the customer dashboard and relevant operational views.
4. Exception states are surfaced with clear next steps when shipping is delayed, failed, or returned.

### 9.3 Business Rules

- Order status must distinguish between created, confirmed, shipped, delivered, returned, canceled, and exception states as appropriate.
- Customers must see the current stage without needing to infer hidden internal processes.
- Status changes must not be communicated as completed while an actual fulfillment or delivery step remains active.

### 9.4 Validation

- Status transitions must be checked against real fulfillment events and shipping provider updates.
- An order must not incorrectly appear delivered, returned, or canceled when the event has not been validly recorded.
- Support and customer interactions must rely on consistent order status semantics across channels.

### 9.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View the current order lifecycle status for their own orders. |
| Admin | Review operational status and override or resolve exceptions when authorized. |
| Artist | Review shipping and fulfillment statuses relevant to their orders. |

### 9.6 Edge Cases

- If fulfillment is paused due to stock, address, or carrier issues, the status must remain accurate and actionable.
- If the package is delivered but the system has not received provider confirmation, the system shall show a pending or exception state rather than a final success.
- If an order is partially fulfilled, the status must reflect that clearly without ambiguity.

### 9.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Every order has a current shipping/fulfillment state that is understandable to the customer.
- Order status is aligned with shipping progress, fulfillment events, and external carrier status where available.
- Exception or delay states are understandable and actionable.

## 10. Returns

### 10.1 Purpose

Returns define how a customer can send back an item or order when it is damaged, incorrect, unwanted under policy, or otherwise eligible for return. The return flow must be clear and fair while preserving operational control.

### 10.2 Workflow

1. Customer requests a return under eligible conditions.
2. System validates order status, return eligibility, time window, and product condition.
3. Customer receives return instructions, label details, or a support path depending on policy.
4. Returned package is received, checked, and processed.
5. Refund or store adjustment is triggered according to the approved return outcome.

### 10.3 Business Rules

- Return eligibility shall be based on approved conditions, order state, and product status.
- Customer communications must clearly explain the return instructions, timing, and any costs or conditions.
- A return must not be marked complete without validation of the actual return or the relevant policy decision.
- Return and refund actions must remain traceable and aligned with the payment and order lifecycle.

### 10.4 Validation

- Return eligibility must be checked against the order, item condition, and policy conditions.
- Items that are ineligible for return must be clearly communicated and blocked.
- Returned item processing must be aligned with refund actions and invoice or settlement updates.

### 10.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Initiate a valid return under supported conditions. |
| Admin | Review and resolve return cases or exceptions. |
| Artist | Support the return and resolution process for their products where policy permits. |

### 10.6 Edge Cases

- If the customer returns an item after the approval window, the system shall reject or route to an exception flow.
- If the item is damaged on return, the case must be assessed under return policy and order conditions.
- If a return is partially approved, the system shall clearly indicate which portion is accepted and how the result affects the payment state.

### 10.7 Acceptance Criteria

- A valid return request results in clear instructions and a grounded outcome.
- Return approval or rejection is consistent across order, payment, and customer communication.
- Return resolution does not silently bypass payment or refund conditions.

## 11. Exchanges

### 11.1 Purpose

Exchanges allow a customer to replace an item with another eligible item when allowed by policy. This supports customer satisfaction while keeping the fulfillment flow operationally controlled.

### 11.2 Workflow

1. Customer requests an exchange for an eligible item.
2. System validates eligibility, item availability, and exchange policy.
3. Customer chooses the replacement option or receives a support path if replacement is unavailable.
4. Exchange is processed, shipped, and confirmed according to order and fulfillment rules.
5. The original and replacement order states remain traceable.

### 11.3 Business Rules

- Exchanges shall only be allowed when the original item is eligible and the replacement is valid under current inventory and policy constraints.
- Exchange instructions and availability must be clearly shown before the customer commits.
- Customer communications must distinguish exchange outcome from return-only or refund-only paths.

### 11.4 Validation

- Replacement availability must be checked before generating the exchange flow.
- Exchange completion must not cause duplicate or conflicting item, payment, or shipment states.
- Exchanges must remain consistent with the original order lifecycle and any applicable return or refund activity.

### 11.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Request an eligible exchange under supported conditions. |
| Admin | Review and resolve exchange exceptions or policy conflicts. |
| Artist | Support exchange-related fulfillment or item availability decisions where allowed. |

### 11.6 Edge Cases

- If the replacement item is unavailable, the system shall offer an alternative or route to support.
- If a return is already in progress, the exchange path must not double-process the original item.
- If the exchange includes a price difference, the customer must be clearly informed before completion.

### 11.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Valid exchanges proceed through a clear item replacement flow.
- Replacement availability and price differences are communicated before completion.
- Exchange activity remains aligned with the original order and payment records.

## 12. Packaging

### 12.1 Purpose

Packaging defines the preparation and presentation of physical items for shipping. It supports safe, efficient delivery while protecting product quality and ensuring the correct shipping method is used.

### 12.2 Workflow

1. Artist or fulfillment team prepares the order for shipment.
2. System or operations workflow confirms packaging requirements based on product, order, and shipping method.
3. Package is sealed, labeled, and finalized for carrier handoff.
4. Order status reflects the packaging state and tracks toward shipment.

### 12.3 Business Rules

- Package preparation must respect item dimensions, fragility, and shipping conditions when applicable.
- Packaging must be suitable for the selected carrier and service level.
- The system must not show a package as shipped if it has not yet been packed and handed off under validated process.

### 12.4 Validation

- Packaging and shipping constraints must align with the product type and order dimensions.
- Unpackaged, mislabeled, or invalid shipment states must be surfaced as exceptions.
- The packaging stage must not bypass the required shipping status checks for the order.

### 12.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Artist | Prepare sellable items for shipment according to approved fulfillment practice. |
| Admin | Monitor packaging exceptions and enforce operational standards. |
| Customer | See the shipping status once packaging is complete and the order is moving toward delivery. |

### 12.6 Edge Cases

- If packaging is delayed, the system shall maintain a valid pending or exception state.
- If an item is fragile or requires special handling, the system must ensure the shipping service is correctly chosen.
- If a package is damaged at fulfillment stage, the order must enter the correct support or replacement path.

### 12.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Orders are only marked as shipped after packaging and handoff requirements are fulfilled.
- Packaging logic reflects the item type and shipping method requirements.
- Fulfillment exceptions are visible and actionable.

## 13. Notifications

### 13.1 Purpose

Shipping notifications keep customers informed about order progress, shipping status changes, delivery issues, and return or exchange updates. They must be timely, relevant, and accessible.

### 13.2 Workflow

1. Shipping state changes occur across fulfillment, carrier handoff, transit, delivery, and return processes.
2. System decides whether to send a notification through the approved communication channel.
3. Customer receives a clear update with the current state and, where helpful, action steps.
4. Notification history remains available in the account or order timeline.

### 13.3 Business Rules

- Notifications shall be sent for meaningful shipping milestones and important exceptions only.
- Notification content must be clear, in the customer’s language, and aligned with the underlying order status.
- Customers must be able to opt into or manage shipping-related notifications where the platform supports preference controls.
- Operational notifications must not overwhelm the customer with duplicate or confusing updates.

### 13.4 Validation

- Shipping notifications must reflect the current state of the shipment and not outdated or stale milestones.
- Customer notification preferences must be respected where allowed by policy.
- High-impact delivery exceptions must trigger a clearer message than routine progress updates.

### 13.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Receive shipping notifications relevant to their orders and configured preferences. |
| Admin | Configure shipping notification templates and review delivery-related alerts. |
| Artist | Receive operational shipping notifications necessary for fulfillment and service quality. |

### 13.6 Edge Cases

- If a shipment is delayed, the customer must receive the reason and recovery or support path when available.
- If notification delivery fails, the order state must remain accurate and the system should retry or schedule a later notification per policy.
- If the customer updates preferences, the shipping system shall honor the latest valid choice.

### 13.7 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers receive meaningful shipping notifications at key milestones.
- Notification content is actionable, localized, and aligned with order status.
- Shipping exceptions produce clear updates without confusing duplicates.

## 14. International Shipping (Future)

### 14.1 Purpose

International shipping enables cross-border delivery when the product, cart, and market support it. This is a future capability and must be introduced only with clear policy, coverage, and support readiness.

### 14.2 Workflow

1. Customer selects a destination outside the domestic shipping region.
2. System validates that international shipping is enabled and the order is eligible.
3. System presents available international options, rates, timings, and restrictions if supported.
4. Customer confirms the order and receives the relevant shipping updates and customs or import context when applicable.
5. Fulfillment and support teams handle any cross-border exception according to policy.

### 14.3 Business Rules

- International shipping must be behind explicit policy approval and market support.
- Rates, zones, and service coverage must be clearly available to the customer before confirmation.
- Customers must be informed of any destination limitations, import duties, or restrictions that may affect the order.
- Cross-border transactions must remain fully aligned with payment, fraud, and fulfillment policies.

### 14.4 Validation

- International shipping must be revalidated against destination, shipment restrictions, and product eligibility.
- Rates and delivery estimates must remain clear and realistic for international delivery windows.
- Operational exceptions such as customs delays or carrier issues must generate an accurate status and customer notification.

### 14.5 Permissions

| Actor | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | View and choose international shipping when enabled and eligible. |
| Admin | Configure and manage international shipping availability and policy. |
| Artist | View international fulfillment status for relevant orders in approved contexts. |

### 14.6 Edge Cases

- If the destination or product is restricted from international shipping, the system shall block checkout or offer a valid alternative.
- If a cross-border shipment is delayed by customs or policy review, the customer must receive the information clearly.
- If a shipping provider cannot serve the destination, the system must show a safe alternative or rejection.

### 14.7 Acceptance Criteria

- International shipping is only shown when explicitly enabled and valid for the order.
- Customers receive clear pricing, timing, and restrictions before purchase.
- Cross-border exceptions are visible and supportable through the shipping lifecycle.

## 15. Cross-Cutting Requirements

### 15.1 Customer Experience

- Shipping details must be presented before payment confirmation in a clear, understandable way.
- Customers must be able to identify the shipping service, cost, timeframe, and current status without confusion.
- Shipping exceptions must use plain-language explanations and next-step guidance.

### 15.2 Operational Excellence

- Admin and fulfillment teams must have a consistent operational view of shipment states, providers, and exceptions.
- Shipping updates must remain aligned with the actual shipping process and external provider signals.
- Shipments requiring intervention must be easy to identify and process through the right support path.

### 15.3 Accessibility and Localization

- Shipping information must support Arabic and English and work correctly in RTL/LTR layouts.
- Customers must be able to understand shipping costs, statuses, and support actions using keyboard and screen-reader accessible flows.
- Delivery and return messages must be clear without requiring unnecessary technical knowledge.

### 15.4 Security and Privacy

- Shipping status and tracking information must respect the customer’s account access and role boundaries.
- Shipment data must not reveal internal operational details beyond what is required for customer visibility.
- Support and admin workflows must protect customer address and order information under the approved privacy and operational controls.

## 16. Acceptance Summary

- Customers can choose valid shipping services with transparent rate and delivery information.
- Orders move through clear shipping states with reliable tracking and actionable customer notifications.
- Returns and exchanges are supported through controlled and policy-safe workflows.
- Packaging and order handling remain aligned with shipping and fulfillment rules.
- International shipping is defined as a future, policy-gated expansion rather than a default baseline capability.
