# Brand Overview

FANNAN is the official digital ecosystem for Palestinian artists and artisans.

Unlike traditional marketplaces, FANNAN combines e-commerce, storytelling, artist portfolios, social interaction, and cultural preservation into one unified platform.

The brand exists to empower Palestinian artists by giving them a professional digital home where they can showcase their work, connect with audiences, and build sustainable creative businesses.

---

# Brand Vision

To become the world's leading digital platform dedicated to Palestinian art and creativity while preserving cultural identity and enabling artists to thrive in the digital economy.

---

# Brand Mission

Create a beautiful, trustworthy, and inspiring digital platform where every Palestinian artist can:

- Showcase their artwork
- Sell original creations
- Receive custom commissions
- Build an international audience
- Preserve cultural heritage through technology

# Brand Story

For generations, Palestinian artists have carried stories through color, embroidery, clay, wood, glass, and stone.

Their creations are more than products.

They are memories.

Identity.

Resistance.

Hope.

Yet many artists still rely on fragmented social media pages to reach customers.

FANNAN was created to change that.

Instead of simply selling art,

FANNAN tells the stories behind it.

Every artist receives a digital home.

Every artwork receives the attention it deserves.

Every buyer becomes part of preserving culture.

# Visual Philosophy

The interface should combine:

Modern technology

+

Traditional craftsmanship

The visual experience should resemble:

A modern art gallery.

Not a discount marketplace.

Design priorities:

- Clean layouts
- Large artwork previews
- Spacious composition
- Premium typography
- Minimal distractions

The artwork—not the interface—should always be the center of attention.

---

# User Experience Philosophy

The user should never wonder:

"What should I click?"

Navigation should feel obvious.

Search should feel intelligent.

Checkout should require minimal effort.

Artists should manage their stores without technical knowledge.

Every interaction should reduce cognitive load.

❌ Invalid input.

# Design Philosophy Summary

When designing any screen, always ask:

- Does this highlight the artwork?
- Does this feel elegant?
- Does this respect Palestinian identity?
- Does this reduce friction?
- Does this help artists succeed?
- Would this experience feel at home in a premium art gallery?

If the answer to any question is "No", redesign the experience.
