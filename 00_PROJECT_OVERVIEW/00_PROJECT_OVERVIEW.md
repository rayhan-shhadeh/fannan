
# Executive Summary

FANNAN is a modern digital marketplace and creative platform dedicated to Palestinian artists and artisans.

The platform connects artists directly with buyers, collectors, businesses, interior designers, cultural organizations, and art lovers through a unified ecosystem consisting of:

- Responsive Website
- Mobile Application (iOS & Android)
- Artist Dashboard
- Customer Dashboard
- Administration Panel

Unlike traditional marketplaces, FANNAN is designed to become the digital home of Palestinian art by combining e-commerce, portfolio management, storytelling, community interaction, and cultural preservation into one experience.

---
