## 1. Overview

This document defines the analytics requirements for FANNAN across marketplace performance, customer behavior, artist business health, operational efficiency, and executive decision making. It covers business metrics, marketplace metrics, artist metrics, customer metrics, order and revenue analytics, traffic, search, product performance, funnels, retention, conversion, and role-based dashboards for admin, artist, and customer users.

The primary goal of analytics is to provide trustworthy, decision-ready measurement across the entire platform while protecting user privacy, maintaining role-based access controls, and supporting bilingual Arabic/English reporting. Analytics shall enable FANNAN to understand acquisition, engagement, conversion, retention, fulfillment, monetization, and operational health without relying on undocumented implementation assumptions.

This document describes product-level measurement requirements only. It does not define database schema, exact event names, telemetry payloads, endpoint contracts, or retention policies. Those details shall be defined in the platform analytics, reporting, and data contract specifications before implementation.

### 3.2 KPI Definitions

The following definitions apply across dashboards unless a more specific localized business policy is documented:

- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV): total value of placed orders before discounts, refunds, and fees.
- Net Revenue: revenue retained after discounts, refunds, payouts, fees, and chargebacks where applicable.
- Conversion Rate: percentage of users who complete a target action, such as purchase, signup, or commission request, relative to eligible sessions or visits.
- Retention Rate: percentage of users or buyers who return and complete a defined activity within a time window.
- AOV (Average Order Value): revenue per completed order across applicable orders.
- Completion Rate: percentage of users reaching the intended end state in a defined funnel stage.
- Bounce Rate: percentage of sessions that did not continue beyond the landing page without meaningful engagement.
- Fill Rate: percentage of customer demand or orders that are successfully fulfilled without cancellation or stock failure.

## 5. Business Analytics

### 5.1 Purpose

Business analytics provide the overall view of FANNAN’s commercial performance and strategic health. These metrics shall support executive reporting, marketplace growth evaluation, and operational planning.

### 5.2 Core Metrics

| Metric | Description | KPI Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GMV | Total value of completed orders before deductions | Primary business KPI |
| Net Revenue | Revenue after discounts, refunds, fees, and adjustments | Primary finance KPI |
| Gross Margin | Revenue less direct fulfillment and processing costs | Primary business KPI |
| Active Sellers | Number of artists with at least one eligible transaction or active listing | Growth KPI |
| Active Buyers | Number of customers with at least one completed purchase or account activity in the period | Growth KPI |
| Order Volume | Total number of orders placed and completed | Operational KPI |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Share of customers placing more than one order | Retention KPI |
| Payout Volume | Total artist payouts and settlements processed | Financial KPI |
| Refund Impact | Refund amount as a percentage of GMV | Risk and quality KPI |
| Conversion to Sale | Percentage of relevant traffic that results in purchase | Funnel KPI |

### 5.3 Business Rules

- Business dashboards must present metrics over comparable periods such as daily, weekly, monthly, and custom date ranges.
- All business KPIs must be traceable to approved event or order data and must reflect the same reporting definitions across channels.
- Exceptions such as refunds, cancellations, and fraud-related adjustments must be visible in the reporting narrative instead of hidden in aggregate totals.
- The system must clearly distinguish between top-line marketplace metrics and net financial metrics.

### 5.4 Validation

- Business metrics must remain consistent across admin dashboard summaries and deeper reports.
- If metric definitions differ by segment or region, the dashboard must explicitly label the scope.
- Revenue values must reconcile to payment and settlement data without unexplained variance.

Marketplace analytics show how the public and discovery experience performs and how demand flows through categories, collections, artists, and product inventory.

### 6.2 Core Metrics

| Metric | Description | KPI Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace Sessions | Total public browsing sessions across marketplace surfaces | Traffic KPI |
| Product Views | Total product-detail views | Discovery KPI |
| Category Views | Total category landing and browse activity | Marketplace KPI |
| Artist Page Views | Views of artist profiles and artist listing pages | Artist discovery KPI |
| Collection Engagement | Views and clicks on featured or curated collections | Discovery KPI |
| Search-to-Product Rate | Share of searches leading to a product click or purchase | Search KPI |
| Browse-to-Add-to-Cart Rate | Percentage of browsing sessions that reach cart | Funnel KPI |

### 6.3 Business Rules

### 6.4 Validation

- Marketplace analytics must not count duplicate actions from repeated refreshes or bot traffic in a way that misstates engagement.
- Product and category performance must reflect eligible public inventory only.
- Regional and locale performance must be viewable without breaking privacy rules.

## 14. Funnel Analytics

### 14.1 Purpose

Funnel analytics define the customer journey from first exposure to purchase completion or other measurable actions. They help identify friction points, conversion drops, and optimization opportunities.

### 14.2 Standard Funnels

| Funnel | Stages |
| --- | --- |
| Marketplace Discovery | Landing page -> category or search -> product detail -> add to cart -> checkout -> order completion |
| Artist Discovery | Profile visit -> product view -> follow or commission request -> purchase or inquiry |
| Search Journey | Query -> result click -> product view -> cart -> checkout | 
| Commission Journey | Profile or listing -> commission request -> submission -> response -> accepted commission |
| Signup Funnel | Landing page -> signup -> verification -> onboarding -> first purchase |

### 14.3 Core Metrics

| Metric | Description | KPI Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Funnel Completion Rate | Percentage of users reaching the end state of a funnel | Conversion KPI |
| Drop-off Rate | Percentage of users leaving at each funnel stage | Quality KPI |
| Time to Completion | Average time from first stage to conversion | Efficiency KPI |
| Stage Conversion | Conversion between each adjacent stage | Funnel KPI |
| Recovery from Drop-off | Re-engagement or return visits after abandonment | Growth KPI |

### 14.4 Business Rules

- Funnel analysis must support multi-step events, stage-level breakout, and source segmentation.
- Abandonment must be visible at the exact stage where users leave the journey.
- Funnel metrics must not be treated as success without considering the business context, such as purchase intent, stock availability, or payment failure.

### 14.5 Validation

- Funnel data must reflect the actual documented user journeys and not hidden internal actions.
- Multiple device or browser sessions from the same user must be tracked in a way that preserves meaningful conversion analysis without crossing privacy boundaries.

| --- | --- |
| Customer | View only their own order, purchase, save, follow, and personal activity analytics. |
| Admin | View customer dashboard metrics only when required for support, compliance, or approved account assistance. |

### 19.4 Validation

- Customer analytics must not expose the activity of other customers or artists.
- Order history and saved-item metrics must match the customer’s documented account actions and order states.
- Customer insights must remain understandable and linked to real account states, not invented recommendations or manipulated engagement scores.

## 20. Reporting and Data Quality Requirements

### 20.1 Standard Reporting Dimensions

All metrics must support, where relevant, the following dimensions:

- Time period: day, week, month, quarter, custom range
- Channel: organic, paid, referral, direct, campaign-based
- Region: city, country, or shipping zone
- Language: Arabic, English, or locale fallback
- Product category: craft, painting, print, sculpture, or existing approved categories
- Artist: seller or creator identity where permitted by permissions
- Customer segment: new, returning, high-value, or VIP customer where policy allows

### 20.2 Data Quality Standards

- Metric definitions must be stable over time and documented within the reporting taxonomy.
- Key metric values must be reconcileable against authoritative event or order sources.
- Missing or delayed data must be clearly represented as incomplete or pending, not silently converted into final values.
- Reporting periods must be labeled to prevent confusion between current, historical, and projected values.

### 20.3 Privacy and Security

- User-level analytics must be restricted to the user’s own account unless an explicit authorized support or admin workflow applies.
- Dashboard exports must protect sensitive personal or financial information according to the documented privacy model.
- Public dashboards and marketplace performance overviews must not reveal private customer or artist identities or unapproved financial details.

## 21. Accessibility and Localization

- Dashboards and analytical views must support Arabic and English, including RTL/LTR layouts and date/number formatting differences.
- Charts, tables, and trends must be readable for keyboard and screen-reader users.
- Color and visual encoding must not be the only way to distinguish KPI states; textual labels and legends must support accessibility.
- Trend comparisons must remain clear when values are negative, zero, or missing.

## 22. Acceptance Summary

- FANNAN includes analytics for business, marketplace, artist, customer, orders, revenue, traffic, search, products, funnels, retention, conversion, and dashboarding.
- The KPIs and metrics are defined at the product level and are aligned to role-based access and business decisions.
- Analytics are presented consistently for admin, artist, and customer contexts without exposing unauthorized data.
- Reporting supports segmentation, quality control, privacy-safe access, and clear operational decision-making.
- The metrics provide a truthful basis for growth, retention, monetization, marketplace quality, and operational improvement.
